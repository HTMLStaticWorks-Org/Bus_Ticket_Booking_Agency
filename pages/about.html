About RouteGo

Engineering the Future of Highway Coach Travel.

Founded with a passion for seamless intercity mobility, RouteGo connects thousands of travelers daily across South & Central India with audited luxury sleeper and seater coaches.

Company Introduction

Company Introduction

RouteGo Bus Ticket Booking Agency Pvt. Ltd. was established to solve the three biggest frustrations in Indian highway bus travel: unpredictable boarding points, opaque ticket surcharges, and inconsistent cabin hygiene.

By integrating directly with verified Volvo B11R, Scania multi-axle, and Mercedes-Benz fleet operators, we give travelers live seat-level inventory, climate-controlled boarding lounge access, and automated refund protection inside a single intuitive platform.

Milestones

Our Journey
