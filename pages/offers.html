Terms & Conditions

  • 1. Eligibility: Promotional codes are open to all travelers booking directly on RouteGo. FIRST25 is exclusively valid on a user's first booking.
  • 2. Validity & Minimum Booking Value: Each coupon specifies an expiry date and minimum base ticket fare (excluding GST and convenience fee) ranging from ₹500 to ₹1,500.
  • 3. Maximum Discount Cap: Percentage-based offers are capped at the stated maximum discount limit (₹250 to ₹500 per PNR).
  • 4. Single Offer Rule: Only one promo code may be applied per booking transaction; offers cannot be combined.
  • 5. Cancellation & Refund Rules: In the event of ticket cancellation, the refund calculation is applied to the actual net amount paid by the traveler after the promotional discount.
