Corridor Briefing

Route Information & Travel Guide

Our flagship Chennai ⇄ Bangalore (NH-48) and Bangalore ⇄ Hyderabad (NH-44) expressways feature six-lane access-controlled carriageways, hygienic midnight food plaza halts, and dedicated city boarding terminals.

Key Boarding Locations

Koyambedu CMBT, Kilambakkam KCBT, Guindy Kathipara, Sriperumbudur, Madiwala Lounge, Majestic, Hebbal, Miyapur.

Major Dropping Points & Stops

Electronic City Toll, Silk Board Junction, Vellore Bypass, Krishnagiri Plaza, Shamshabad Airport Road, Lakdikapul.

Highway Distance: 348 km (NH-48) / 569 km (NH-44) • Average Speed: 65–75 km/h • Rest Stop: 20-min verified hygiene plaza

Policy & Fee Transparency

Fare & Booking Information

Clear, upfront guidelines on ticket pricing, taxes, payment options, cancellation slabs, and refund processing.

Pricing & Convenience Fees

Displayed base fares include highway toll charges. A nominal 5% GST and ₹35 platform service fee apply at checkout.

Payment Information

Instant confirmation via UPI (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm), Visa/Mastercard/RuPay cards, Net Banking, and wallets.

Cancellation Charges

Only 15% cancellation fee when cancelled more than 24 hours before departure; 25% between 12 and 24 hours.

Refund Conditions

100% full refund if an operator cancels a service. Standard refunds credit to original payment source in 3–5 business days.
