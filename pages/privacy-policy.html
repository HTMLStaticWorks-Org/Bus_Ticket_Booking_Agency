Data Governance

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2026 • Covering Data Collection, Personal Information, Data Protection, and Cookies.

1. Data Collection

When you search routes or book bus tickets on RouteGo, we collect only the minimum operational information required to issue your boarding pass and deliver live coach telemetry: passenger names, age, gender (for female-safe seat adjacency compliance), contact mobile number, and email address.

2. Personal Information

Your personal information is shared strictly with the verified bus operator executing your specific PNR journey for boarding verification and highway emergency alerts. We never sell or rent traveler contact lists to third-party advertisers.

3. Data Protection

All checkout sessions are encrypted via TLS 1.3 and PCI-DSS compliant payment tokenization. Card numbers, CVV codes, and UPI PINs are never stored on RouteGo servers.

4. Cookies & Local Storage

RouteGo uses essential browser storage (localStorage and sessionStorage) to remember your Dark/Light theme preference, RTL layout setting, active seat selection session, and demo Traveler Dashboard bookings.
